COMPLETE WALKTHROUGH

Chapter-by-chapter solutions and choice guides

📺 EPISODE 1: FAITH

Chapter 1: The Crooked Man's Office

  • • Talk to the Crooked Man about the murder
  • • Choice: How you greet him affects Bigby's reputation
  • • Travel to the scene of the crime

Chapter 2: Crime Scene Investigation

  • • Examine the decapitated body (Faith)
  • • Collect evidence: Gold button, whiskey glass, bloody sheet
  • • Talk to nearby witness (Lily)
  • • Choice: Believe Lily or interrogate further

Chapter 3: The Trip Trap

  • • Visit the Trip Trap bar to find Nun
  • • Meet Tatterdamalion - obtain key information
  • • Fight sequence - use claw sparingly
  • • Choice: Let attacker escape or pursue

Chapter 4: Interview Mr. Grumpy

  • • Visit the Woodsman's apartment
  • • Confront him about the gold button
  • • Choice: Threaten or reason with him
  • • Investigate the mirror - discover Crane's involvement

Chapter 5: The Butcher's Shop

  • • Find the murder weapon at the butcher's
  • • Confront Crane about his secret
  • • Choice: Expose Crane or keep his secret
  • • Episode 1 ends - Lily transforms

📺 EPISODE 2: SMOKE & MIRRORS

Chapter 1: The Farm

  • • Travel to the Farm to find answers about Lily
  • • Meet Bluebeard and Bloody Mary
  • • Explore the Farm's secrets
  • • Choice: Trust Bloody Mary or not

Chapter 2: The Witch

  • • Find the Witch's cottage
  • • Learn about the three murders
  • • Discover Crane's connection to the murders
  • • Choice: Share information or keep secrets

⚖️ KEY CHOICES THAT MATTER

Crane's Secret

Keeping Crane's secret builds trust with him later. Exposing him creates an enemy.

Faith's Killer

Your accusation at the end affects the final case outcome.

Bigby's Humanity

Using Wolf powers increases fear but may help in fights.

Snow White Relationship

How you treat Snow affects her trust and the story ending.
