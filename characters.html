CHARACTERS

The Fabletown residents you'll meet on your investigation

🐺 MAIN CHARACTERS

🐺

Bigby Wolf

The Big Bad Wolf / Sheriff of Fabletown

Former antagonist of fairy tales, now reformed and serving as Fabletown's sheriff. Struggles to control his violent nature while maintaining order in the community.

Played by: Public opinion based on your choices
❄️

Snow White

Fabletown's Mayor / Bigby's ally

The de facto leader of Fabletown. Bigby's closest friend and former lover. Works tirelessly to protect the community despite political pressure.

Relationship: Trust level affected by your choices

👥 FABTOWN RESIDENTS

🎩

Bluebeard

Wealthy Fable, Businessman

Obsessed with accumulating wealth. Has a hidden vault of trophies from his past wives.

🗡️

Ichabod Crane

Fabletown's Governor / The Woodcutter

Spineless politician with a dark secret. His corruption runs deeper than anyone knows.

👠

Beauty

Beauty and the Beast

Now transformed into a spider due to Beast's curse. Runs the Trip Trap bar.

🪓

The Woodsman

Little Red's protector

Simple but loyal. Works grueling jobs to stay in Fabletown. Has a gold button connecting him to the murders.

🐷

Flynn

Pig Fable, Bartender

Works at the Trip Trap. Knows everyone's business but keeps quiet.

🧙

Nun

Sister of the Opportune

Seeks redemption through religion. Connected to Faith's past.

💀 ANTAGONISTS

🎭

The Crooked Man

Crime Boss of Fabletown

The shadowy mastermind controlling Fabletown's underworld. Runs the Trenchies and the city's darkest secrets. Not what he seems.

🩸

Bloody Mary

The Witch / Nursery Rhyme Killer

A terrifying Fable who kills those connected to Nursery Rhymes. Has a personal vendetta against Snow White.
