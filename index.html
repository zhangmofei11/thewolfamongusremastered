🎬 ABOUT THE GAME

The Premise

Before the events of the Fables comic series, Bigby Wolf - formerly the Big Bad Wolf - serves as the sheriff of Fabletown, a secret community of fairy tale characters living in exile in the mundane world. When the Crooked Man summons Bigby to the Blood Farm, a brutal murder investigation begins that will change Fabletown forever.
