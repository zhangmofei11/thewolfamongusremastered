Every decision shapes your story and determines the ending

📺 EPISODE 1: FAITH

Greeting the Crooked Man

Aggressive approach

Sets tone as tough sheriff. Crooked Man respects power.

Professional approach

Neutral start. Keeps options open.

The Woodsman's Gold Button

Accuse him directly

Confrontation. He may reveal information under pressure.

Show him the evidence

Subtle approach. Builds trust, he opens up more.

Crane's Secret

Expose Crane

Creates enemy. Snow loses trust in Crane.

Keep his secret

Crane becomes ally. Can use his information later.

📺 EPISODE 2: SMOKE & MIRRORS

Bloody Mary

Trust Bloody Mary

She provides key information. May come back to haunt you.

Distrust her

Play it safe. Miss some information but stay cautious.

The Farm Massacre

Kill the Farm Officer

Brutal but effective. Bigby's humanity decreases.

Spare him

Mercy shown. Preserves more of Bigby's humanity.

🏆 ENDINGS

The Justiciar Ending

Bigby maintains strict moral code. The community respects him but some see him as weak.

The Sheriff Ending

Bigby uses fear and power to maintain order. Feared but effective.

The Reformed Ending

Bigby reconciles with his past. Becomes a true protector of Fabletown.

The Lost Ending

Bigby succumbs to his darker nature. The community fears him above all.
